## 11. Factual Coleman Brand Note (For Agent A's Brand About System)
*(Agent A owns `lib/brand-info.ts` and brand profile UI; this factual summary is provided for their integration)*

> **Brand:** Coleman (The Coleman Company, Inc., Newell Brands)  
> **Founded:** 1900 in Kingfisher, Oklahoma by W.C. Coleman; headquartered in Chicago, Illinois.  
> **Heritage & Field Role:** Originating with the invention of the pressurized gasoline lantern that transformed rural and outdoor illumination, Coleman became the foundational American manufacturer of portable outdoor camp gear, liquid-fuel cooking appliances, and expedition shelters. During World War II, the iconic Coleman G.I. Pocket Stove (Model 520) established military-grade portable field cooking. In forestry, ecological research, and wilderness exploration, Coleman equipment provides mission-critical camp infrastructure—spanning weather-tested all-season cabin tents, sub-zero sleeping systems, instant canopy workstations, rugged cots, and dual-fuel expedition stoves that operate reliably in remote off-grid locations where standard fuels are inaccessible.
